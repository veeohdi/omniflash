OmniFlash is designed specifically for the Google Pixel 4 XL (codename: coral).

Flashing firmware carries an inherent risk of bricking your device if interrupted or misused.

  • Use only official Google factory images downloaded directly from developers.google.com.
  • Always use a high-quality, reliable USB cable connected directly to your computer (avoid unpowered hubs).
  • Ensure your phone has at least 30% battery.
  • Flashing will completely wipe all user data on the device.
